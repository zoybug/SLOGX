Primary source: Smart_Logistics_Class_Intelligence_Phase1-3.xlsx, supplied with the handoff.
Unit: 30 unique Assignment 01 submissions. A duplicate appearance identified in the workbook methodology was counted once.

Co-occurrence: AI + Optimization 17/30; IoT + Digital Twin 15/30. A co-mention is not proof of causation.
Geography: 11 named example geographies. One submission has geography not specified. These are locations of cited examples, not inferred student nationalities.
Q4: Future themes are described qualitatively. The dashboard contains analyst-coded aggregates but the supplied workbook has no row-level Q4 indicator matrix to independently reconstruct them.
Q2: Dashboard totals differ from a literal split of the Master Dataset because categories include wording variants and repeated labels. No Q2 count is presented in the deliverables.
Cases: All nine displayed cases trace to Master Dataset rows. They are student-cited topics; dates, operational status and measured outcomes were not externally checked.
Quotes: Anonymous standout insights reproduce the workbook's analyst-coded summaries, not verified verbatim text from original student files.
No external sources were used.
