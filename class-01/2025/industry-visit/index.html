SF Express Shenzhen

Industry Visit · 21 November 2025 · Shenzhen Heji transit center
Host: Prof. Wai Kin (Victor) Chan

Visit overview

On 21 November 2025, the class visited SF Express’s Shenzhen Heji transit center with Prof. Wai Kin (Victor) Chan. The tour followed how parcel records, measurement and sorting equipment support onward dispatch. The discussion with the branch manager covered handling priorities, staff performance, exceptions, technology and internships. The account below follows the organizer’s visit summary; reported processing times are the manager’s explanations, rather than measurements made by the class.

Visit account

Parcel identification and sorting

Barcode scanning connects the physical parcel to its record. Dimensions help determine suitable handling, and diverters, cross-belt and tilt-tray systems direct parcels to the next lane. The stage explorer gives a simplified account of the tour rather than an equipment specification.

Identify the parcel

Barcode scanners on the conveyor lines connect each parcel with the information needed for its next movement.

Course connection: a physical parcel and its digital record must stay aligned.

Handling requirements and processing time

Standard and fast-tracked perishable services were described with different handling priorities. The reported 30–40-minute complete hub cycle and under-5-minute perishable handling have different boundaries. These descriptions cannot establish a measured speed advantage or a universal processing time.

Staff-reported processing time30–40 minutes

A complete hub cycle

Arrival, sorting, repackaging and outbound dispatch formed the cycle described by the branch manager.

Ask what starts and ends the clock, which parcels are included, and how peak periods affect the result.

Staff, exceptions and responsibilities

Accuracy, productivity and safety were discussed alongside the handling of exception parcels. The manager also described in-house technology, internships and selective partnerships. The career answers record a November 2025 conversation and are not a current recruitment offer.

How is staff performance measured?

The manager described accuracy and productivity alongside safety practices and the handling of exception parcels.

A useful measure captures both the volume of work and the quality of the handoff.
