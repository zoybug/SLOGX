## Actual assignment

This archive adds synthesis and practice scenarios for review. It does not introduce a new assessment rubric. No grades or student-level feedback appear here.

## Suggested review questions (editorial extensions)

1. What service and users does the application support?
2. Which observations feed which decision, and who carries it out?
3. What makes the plan feasible: time, capacity, energy, infrastructure or permissions?
4. Which claims are demonstrated, modeled, proposed or independently measured?
5. What baseline and system boundary would establish a useful improvement?
