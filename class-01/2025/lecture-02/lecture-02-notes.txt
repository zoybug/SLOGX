Electric Vehicles-Enabled Transportation
Classification of charging technology
• Battery replacement
•
•
•

Battery swapping is one of the efficient and fast charging methods. Through battery swapping stations (BSS), electric vehicle owners can
easily replace depleted batteries with fully charged new ones.
BSS includes distribution transformers, AC/DC converters, battery chargers, robotic arms, charging racks, maintenance systems, control
systems, and other equipment, aiming to provide battery exchange and charging services.
From the perspective of the power grid, the variable demand of BSS makes it a flexible resource. When the power grid is under high
pressure, BSS can inject electricity into the power system to balance the load demand of the grid and provide services to the grid.

Truck-and-Drone Delivery
➢ Multi-trucks-and-drones cooperative pickup and delivery problem
This study aims to propose a decision methodology on scheduling trucks and drones for truck-and-drone cooperative
delivery and pickup system. A fleet contains multiple truck groups; each truck group is a truck with carrying multiple
drones. The fleet serves a set of dispersed customers who have the requirements of pickup and delivery services as well as

Drone-Truck Surveillance
➢ Nested vehicle routing problem: Optimizing drone-truck surveillance operations
Unmanned aerial vehicles or drones are becoming increasingly popular due to their low cost and high mobility. In this
paper we address the routing and coordination of a drone-truck pairing where the drone travels to multiple locations to
perform specified observation tasks and rendezvous periodically with the truck to swap its batteries. We refer to this as the
Nested-Vehicle Routing Problem (Nested-VRP) and develop a Mixed Integer Quadratically Constrained Programming
(MIQCP) formulation with critical operational constraints, including drone battery capacity and synchronization of both
vehicles during scheduled rendezvous.

Zeng, F., Chen, Z., Clarke, J. P., & Goldsman, D. (2022). Nested vehicle routing problem: Optimizing drone-truck
surveillance operations. Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies, 139, 103645.
Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School, Institute of Data and Information, Prof. Mingyao Qi

–

◼ Heuristics
–

–

A heuristic algorithm is one that is designed to solve a problem in a faster and more
efficient fashion than traditional methods by sacrificing optimality, accuracy,
precision, or completeness for speed.
Heuristic is often greedy, myopic, or rule-based.

◼ Metaheuristics
–

–
–

a higher-level procedure designed to find, generate, or select a heuristic (partial
search algorithm) that may provide a sufficiently good solution to an optimization
problem.
Can temporarily accept worse or even infeasible solutions.
For example, Genetic Algorithm, Tabu Search, Variable Neighborhood Search.
