Evidence and limits

Read algorithms, demonstrations and environmental claims carefully.

01 / METHODS

A solver needs a defined problem.

  • Shortest path is one leg. VRP adds assignment, stop sequence and fleet constraints.
  • Exact methods can prove optimality for their model when solved with a valid bound. “100 customers” is not a universal cutoff.
  • Construction heuristics build a starting plan; 2-opt/3-opt improve route edges. Metaheuristics explore alternatives.
  • Clarke–Wright savings: c(0,i) + c(0,j) − c(i,j). Consider larger savings first; merge only compatible, feasible routes.
  • Learning alone does not guarantee a feasible dispatch.

02 / EVIDENCE

Name the status of the claim.

  • CityFlow Connect is a student concept. The forum-described visibility platform is not verified as a deployed product.
  • The airborne warehouse is a patent/concept; a flight demonstration does not establish routine passenger service.
  • Payload and endurance are vehicle-specific. Passenger eVTOL and parcel UAV limits differ.
  • Electric vehicles have no tailpipe exhaust at use; net impacts depend on the energy and lifecycle boundary.
  • Wireless charging transfers supplied energy. It does not create free energy.
Read a controlled wireless charging demonstration ↗
Before repeating a saving or safety claim: check the date, scale, comparator, constraints and measurement. Broad cost shares, free swapping, universal driverless platoons and general safety gains are not established by this corpus.

These clarifications are editorial teaching support informed by the supplied lecture and primary readings. No instructor feedback was supplied or invented.
