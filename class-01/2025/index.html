Smart Logistics.

Explore the Fall 2025 lectures and the questions students raised about them. The course, taught by Prof. Wai Kin (Victor) Chan and guest faculty, covers how data, technology and operating decisions shape logistics. Six of the seven scheduled lectures are available below.

ARCHIVE / LECTURE 01 · 15 SEPTEMBER 2025

Digital Transformation of Modern Supply Chains

Start with supply-chain history and AI readiness, then compare three cases: fresh-food procurement, Flexport and S.F. Holding. Reflections from 30 students add questions about automation, sustainability and implementation.
