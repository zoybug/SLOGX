Introduction to Smart Logistics

Introduction to Smart Logistics examines the technologies, analytical methods and operating decisions used to move, store and coordinate goods. Prof. Wai Kin (Victor) Chan leads the course, with invited academics and industry practitioners teaching sessions in their specialist areas.
