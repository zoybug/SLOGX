From trade to transformation

Three main themes from the guest lecture.

About this summary ↓

Trade foundations

Trade connects exchange with the work of moving goods.

  • Ko began with global trade and barter as a starting point for understanding exchange.
  • He then introduced supply chain management and logistics: the coordination of a supply chain and the physical movement of goods.
Trade
Exchanging goods and services across borders.
Barter
Exchanging goods or services directly, without money.
Supply chain management
Coordinating activities from sourcing to delivery.
Logistics
Moving goods through that system.

Platforms and growth

Technology platforms open new opportunities for business growth.

  • The lecture moved from logistics foundations to the platforms driving the modern economy.
  • Ko emphasized the opportunity for companies to rise and grow in this changing environment.

The setting for the talk

The event abstract placed these opportunities within changes in globalization:

  • Trade tensions and pandemic disruption
  • E-commerce and attention to ESG
  • Technology and digitalization
From the event poster ↗

The Flexport example

The Flexport example brought several sides of global trade together.

  • Ko compared the challenges facing banks, merchants and logistics providers.
  • He then explained the Flexport model as a response to those challenges.
