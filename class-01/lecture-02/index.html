PROF. QI'S ROUTING TOOLKIT
Construct

Nearest neighbor or Clarke-Wright savings builds a starting route.

Improve

2-opt tests whether swapping route edges reduces travel while preserving constraints.

Validate

Capacity, time windows, energy, and safety decide whether a shorter route can actually run.

The class raised this distinction in ride-hailing, courier batching, electric vehicles, drone stations, and UPS stop sequencing. See Grab's allocation explanation and UPS on ORION versus UPSNav.

STUDENT CONTENT / 04 OF 05LECTURE 02

Benefit or proof?

The class found plausible advantages. The evidence label tells us how firmly to state them.

A / DEMONSTRATED

Observed operation

A dated deployment with a defined unit: trips, delivery time, route miles, or a documented process.

B / COMPANY CLAIM

Reported benefit

A supplier or operator reports an effect. Record whose claim it is and the comparison baseline.

C / MODELLED

Study result

A simulation or experiment suggests a gain under stated assumptions. Do not call it a live service result.

D / PROPOSED

Future design

A useful idea—such as joint charging and dispatch—can be analyzed without saying it is already deployed.

Scope changes meaning.Wing's million-plus figure is cumulative commercial deliveries, not deliveries per year. Grab's company revenue is not a dispatch-effect measure. Zipline's reported 51% finding concerns in-hospital deaths due to postpartum hemorrhage, not all maternal deaths.

Check the underlying Wing update, Grab results, and Zipline's study summary.

THE NEXT 5–10 YEARS / STUDENT OUTLOOK

Students proposed more predictive dispatch, mixed human and autonomous fleets, and tighter air-ground or port coordination. The strongest shared condition is trustworthy data joined to feasible decisions, with safety and worker impacts measured alongside speed.

ANONYMOUS CLASS INSIGHT · PARAPHRASED

A route with a believable arrival promise can be better than a nominally faster route that repeatedly misses it.
