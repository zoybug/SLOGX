# Fall 2025 content and interaction standard

This source-led 2025 reading view supersedes the older slide-view guidance in
EDITORIAL_STYLE.md for 2025 pages. The 2026 implementation is unchanged.

Read the actual assignment and lecture material before rewriting a session.
Official detailed briefs/decks establish the delivered lecture title; the actual
guest event poster supersedes the tentative seventh syllabus entry. Do not
reconstruct an assignment when no student work was supplied.

Each lecture opens with quiet number/date/presenter metadata, its full official
title. The overview and Lecturer profile section stay visible, including the full
biography and one original official profile link. The archive contains a course
introduction and one chronological 2025 Sessions list. A single, number-free introduction is
used for Student synthesis because enrollment of 31 was not verified.

Put the genuine assignment before its findings. Lecture 01 has four questions;
Lectures 02, 03, 05 and 06 have one report/reflection task. Lecture 04 has one report
with five optional suggested aspects. Keep common themes and differing views,
without treating overlapping keyword counts as votes or measured learning gains.

Distinguish lecture material, student perspectives, editorial interpretation,
constructed teaching models and independent references. Explain inputs, outputs,
units and limits before each model. Native More detail dialogs retain original
task wording, meaningful diagrams, calculations, caveats and evidence boundaries.
They must support Escape, keyboard scrolling, focus return and page-state retention.

Use one official profile link near the top and compact references near the bottom.
Internal reference links consolidate URLs. Date external claims honestly; a later
company source does not establish what happened during the 2025 event or visit.
Visit processing times are staff-reported descriptions with different boundaries.

Practice quizzes use realistic scenarios and plausible alternatives of comparable
length. Each question has one defensible answer; the 12 keys per quiz are balanced
without a repeating position sequence. Explanations state the relevant reasoning
and source. Scoring and teaching calculations remain separate from presentation.

content-2025.js guards an answered, incomplete attempt on supported link navigation,
same-document history and Restart. Stay/Escape preserve state; Leave resets it and
resumes the intended destination. Refresh, tab close and cross-document browser
history use native beforeunload where supported. Unstarted and completed attempts
do not prompt. Multiple dialogs and navigation must not cause scroll or focus loops.

The Editorial Atelier layer, atelier-2025.css, follows content-2025.css only on
2025 pages. Keep the original topic palettes, Source Serif 4 headings, Source
Sans 3 text and warm ivory canvas. Use a centered 1000px composition with a
720px reading column, naturally spaced left-aligned prose, fine dividers and
controlled whitespace. Reserve bounded white surfaces for functional controls,
teaching models, charts and dialogs. The homepage uses compact typographic rows;
all eight session destinations remain in a native Sessions menu.

Show each genuine assignment question or thematic finding with a faithful short
preview and a native inline Read full analysis disclosure. Keep the entire existing
analysis, evidence, qualifications and charts inside it. Multiple items may remain
open; no exclusive accordion or fixed-height narrative scroll area is allowed.
Number only actual assignment questions. More detail dialogs remain secondary.
Load atelier-2025.js before content-2025.js so accepted deep-link/history navigation
reveals the relevant analysis before scrolling without bypassing the quiz guard.

Retain all meaningful model diagrams, the authentic guest poster and the original
transparent overview illustrations, presented quietly. These illustrations are
decorative, not photographic evidence of a lecture or visit. The superseded
rectangles-2025.css is no longer loaded. Inspect computed typography and test at
wide desktop, tablet and 390px, with narrow-screen and zoom checks. Verify actual
deployed pages and assets after Pages builds.

The shared meerkat-companion layer shows a natural-looking car-mechanic meerkat
immediately wherever the fact companion is present. Use adult animal proportions,
small natural eyes and a tapered muzzle with practical mechanic workwear.
There is no orb, evolution, transformation, aura or mascot animation.
Every companion click reveals a sourced fact. Keep the fact panel focused on the
fact and controls, with no cosmic or Mars discussion. Preserve all 100 facts and
the existing discovery storage. Ignore old evolution progress. Keep keyboard
controls and safe placement around academic controls intact.
