Lecture 01 / 15 September 2026

Digital Transformation of Modern Supply Chains

Fresh-food buying, freight coordination and network planning pose different decisions. The class used these cases to ask when a forecast can change what someone orders, shares or ships.

Course instructorProf. Wai Kin (Victor) Chan

Wai Kin (Victor) Chan is a professor at Tsinghua University and the course instructor. His research includes simulation, optimization and statistical analysis, with applications in transportation and other complex systems.

THE LEAD / Q4 OUTLOOK

The forecast is only the beginning.

Students envisioned logistics that can anticipate demand and disruption, then adjust the plan while goods are still moving. The sharper question is whether a prediction reaches a feasible action—and whether the result teaches the network something useful.
