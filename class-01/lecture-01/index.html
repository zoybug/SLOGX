Class atlas / Lecture 01

Digital Transformation of Modern Supply Chains

A short history of supply chains and AI led into a practical question: what must be ready before intelligent systems can help a real logistics network? Fresh food, Flexport and S.F. Holding gave the class three different settings in which to test that question.
